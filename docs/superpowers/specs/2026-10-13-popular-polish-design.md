# Cardwar "make it popular" — design

Date 2026-10-13. Follows the MVP umbrella spec (`2026-10-12-mvp-design.md`); everything there still holds.
Owner brief (approved in chat, executed autonomously overnight): polish the MVP into a game Ukrainians open
daily and show friends. Three fronts: game feel, satire content about public figures, and retention hooks
(shop people crave, streaks, missions, ranks, rematch).

## 1. What the play-test found (2026-10-13, Telegram Web K, two real accounts + a scripted opponent)

Works: sign-in, invite by stake, share sheet, join, full duel over SSE, strikes and forfeit, coins settle,
history, friends from results, bot messages. The backbone is sound.

Fix (feel):

- F1 On a narrow viewport (Telegram Web's 330 px popup, small phones) a hand of 8+ cards overflows both
  edges. Card width must follow the hand width, not a fixed token.
- F2 The result screen is a bare card ("Поразка", reason, −100, "До початку"): no emotion, no rematch,
  no record, no streak. This is the single most important screen for retention.
- F3 The profile header shows the internal `@tg_768247286` instead of the Telegram handle (or nothing).
- F4 The table is flat: discard pile is a text line, the pot is a number, the empty felt is a plain
  rectangle, nothing marks whose turn it is besides the status line.
- F5 On Telegram Web the Mini App opens as a small popup; the app never asks to expand.
- F6 The 30 s clock is right (Durak Online uses the same), but a player has no idea it exists until the
  bar is red. The first own turn gets a one-time hint toast.

Not a bug (checked in code and logs): strikes reset on a real move; the pairs of timeouts 31 s apart in the
log were the tester's own latency, not a stale deadline.

## 2. Content: satire packs

### Boundaries (binding, regardless of permissions given)

Public figures only; jokes about public conduct in the register of the «Smak» channel (deadpan fake
headline + absurd quote). No sexual content, no ethnic or religious slurs, no private persons, no real
photos or recordings, no fabricated factual claims presented as news inside the app beyond obvious parody
(the pack is named satire in the shop and on the pack face). Mild profanity at Smak level is fine. No
jokes about mobilisation evasion, casualties or captivity.

### Art and voice reality

We own no likenesses. Faces are **poster cards**: persona name in display type, role line, signature
emoji badge, persona colour, one catchphrase per card (so 36 distinct quotes per deck), suit and rank
in both corners large enough to play by, and a small «САТИРА» tag. Generated by a script from a roster
file so real caricatures drop into the same files later.

Voices: no cloning. A new rule effect `say` reads a line with the device's Ukrainian TTS
(`speechSynthesis`, `uk-UA`); synthesized stingers for sounds. Recorded voice clips replace both later
through the same slots.

  `uk-UA` voice when available, else any voice; obeys the sound setting and the 150 ms de-dupe; cancels

  bubble on `table` with the same text. `src/audio/speech.ts`, injectable for tests.

- `packs:check` stays the gate; both packs validate strictly.

## 3. Shop people want

Changeset 013 extends `shop_item`:

```
unlock_kind     VARCHAR(16) NOT NULL DEFAULT 'COINS'   -- COINS | WIN_STREAK
unlock_value    INT         NOT NULL DEFAULT 0         -- streak length for WIN_STREAK
drop_slot       INT         NULL                       -- weekly drop: on sale when isoWeek % 4 = slot
```

A WIN_STREAK item costs 0 coins and is granted (owned) when `GET /shop` sees the player's best win streak
≥ `unlock_value`; the view says `unlock: { kind, value, met }`. A `drop_slot` item is purchasable only
during its week (Kyiv time); the view carries `availableUntil` (ISO) for the countdown and the shop shows
the drop first with «Дроп тижня» and a countdown; outside its week it is shown locked with «Повернеться».

Seed (names are the shop copy; refs are CSS/pack ids):

- Decks: `deck-sluhy-narodu` 1500 coins; `deck-blogery` WIN_STREAK 3 (earned, the one people grind for).
- Backs: `back-bayraktar` 400, `back-palianytsia` 300, `back-ukrzaliznytsia` 300, `back-bavovna` 500
  (drop slot 0), `back-chornobaivka` 450 (drop slot 2).
- Tables: `table-blackout` 600 («Хрещатик при блекауті», dark with window lights), `table-bukovel` 600,
  `table-kabmin` 800 (drop slot 1), `table-metro` 700 (drop slot 3). Every week slot has a drop.
- Frames: `frame-tryzub-gold` 900, `frame-vyshyvanka` 700, `frame-kozak` WIN_STREAK 5.
- Emotes (phrase emotes: glyph + text bubble): `emote-minus-money` «Мінус гроші» 150,
  `emote-chumachecha` «Чумачеча осінь» 150, `emote-ty-sho` «Ти шо, Кличко?» 200,

## 4. Retention hooks

### Progress (account module, `player_progress` gains `win_streak`, `best_win_streak`, `xp`; changeset 014)

- On duel completion (already routed through the account service): winner streak +1, loser streak 0,
  draw keeps both; best updated. XP: win 30 (+10 if stake > 0), loss 10, draw 15.
- Ranks by XP: Дурак 0, Підкидний 100, Козир 300, Шулер 700, Легенда 1500.
- `GET /api/v1/me/progress` → `{ duelsPlayed, duelsWon, winStreak, bestWinStreak, xp, rank,
  nextRankXp | null, streakBonusPercent }` where `rank` ∈ `DURAK|PIDKYDNYI|KOZYR|SHULER|LEHENDA`
  (client maps to Ukrainian), `streakBonusPercent = 10 * min(winStreak, 5)` = the bonus the *next* staked
  win pays.

### Streak bonus (monetization)

On settling a staked duel the winner also receives `stake * 10% * min(streakBeforeThisWin, 5)` from the
house as `CoinKind.STREAK_BONUS` (ledger reference `duel:<id>:streak`). Idempotent with settle.

### Daily missions (monetization; table `daily_mission`, changeset 014)

Three per Kyiv day, same for everyone: `PLAY_3` (play 3 duels, 60 coins), `WIN_2` (120),
`STAKED_WIN_1` (win one duel with stake ≥ 100, 150). Progress is recorded when a duel completes.
`GET /api/v1/me/missions` → `[{ code, target, progress, reward, claimed }]`;
`POST /api/v1/me/missions/{code}/claim` → wallet view (400 `MISSION_INCOMPLETE`, 409 `ALREADY_CLAIMED`).
Ledger kind `MISSION_REWARD`, reference `mission:<day>:<code>`.

### Friends leaderboard (session)

`GET /api/v1/friends/leaderboard` → `[{ player: PlayerProfileView, wins, coinsWon, you }]` over the last
7 days among the caller's friends plus the caller, by coinsWon desc then wins, top 20.

### Rematch (client, existing APIs)

The result screen offers «Реванш» = a targeted challenge to the opponent at the same stake (the opponent
is a friend as soon as the result is stored). While a result screen is open the client polls incoming
challenges every 5 s and shows «{name} кличе на реванш» with Прийняти. The challenger lands on the
waiting screen as today.

### Streak reminder (monetization → bot through a port, like payments)

Daily at 19:00 Kyiv: every player with a Telegram id whose daily-bonus streak ≥ 2 and no claim today gets
one DM: «🔥 Серія {n} днів згорить опівночі. Забери бонус» with a Play button. One per day per player,
recorded so a restart does not resend.

## 5. Client: screens that change

- Lobby: streak flame chip («Серія 3 · +30% до виграшу») when winStreak ≥ 1; missions card (three
  rows with progress bars and Забрати); drop-of-the-week teaser linking to the shop.
- Result screen v2: hero word with motion (confetti burst / coin shower on win; vignette + shake on loss;
  neutral on draw), opponent avatar + «проти {name}» + head-to-head record, coin delta count-up, streak
  line («Серія 4 🔥» or «Серію перервано»), XP gained and rank-up banner when crossed, mission progress
  lines that changed, buttons «Реванш» (primary) and «До початку». Sounds win/lose already exist.
- Table: discard pile as a fanned stack of backs with a count badge; pot as a chip stack; a soft glow on
  whoever's avatar is to move; opponent fan centred; first own turn shows a one-time clock hint.
- Profile: handle fix (`@handle` or nothing), rank badge and XP bar, «Топ друзів» list (leaderboard).
- Shop: locked items with requirement badge, drop section with countdown, phrase emotes preview.
- Settings: unchanged (sound setting also gates `say`).
- Launch: `expand()` after `ready()`.

## 7. Visual identity (MVP 12, 2026-10-14)

Owner feedback after MVP 8–11: «design looks little weak». One branded, always-dark look replaces the
Telegram-theme-driven flat UI:

- Palette «нічний Хрещатик»: ink `#0b0d12` with a faint dot lattice, gold for money and the main action, azure for
  the secondary action, emerald felt with a wood-and-gold rim. The app no longer reads `--tg-theme-*`; Telegram's
  header and background are painted in the ink (`setHeaderColor`/`setBackgroundColor`).
- Type: Unbounded (display, numbers, wordmark) and Manrope (body), self-hosted under `client/public/fonts`
  (OFL, cyrillic + latin variable subsets, ~125 KB); `kit/fonts.css`, checked by a test.
- Components: panels with a lit edge, buttons with a solid bottom edge that sink on press (gold primary, azure
  secondary), glass bottom bar, gold status pill when it is your move, fanned hand (tilt capped so a full hand still
  fits 320 px), lobby brand strip + one felt play panel, profile hero, shop cards with fanned previews.
- Classic deck v2 (`scripts/gen-cards.mjs`): cream paper, redrawn pips, court cards as mirrored flat figures,
  ornament aces, medallion back. File names unchanged.
- Satire posters v2 (`scripts/lib/poster.mjs`): a `look` per persona (hair, beard, glasses, clothes; emoji badge)
  renders a generic cartoon bust, on a gradient-and-halftone panel with a tilted «САТИРА» stamp. Boundaries in §2
  unchanged: no likeness, no photos; recognition comes from the name and the badge.
- Cosmetics: drawn backs get gloss and a bigger shop sample, table swatches wear the felt's rim, frames cast a shadow.

Real illustrated art and recorded voices remain the owner's call; the slots are unchanged.
